# REHEARSAL — FAKE PROVIDERS — NOT A QUALITY RESULT

This directory is a rehearsal of the benchmark machinery with the
deterministic fake providers. The fake reranker sorts by lexical
score and understands no language; the fake embedder hashes
character trigrams and cannot match a paraphrase.

Nothing in `report.md` is a statement about mapping quality. It
exists to show that the instrument produces what it claims to
produce. See `report.md` for the run itself.
